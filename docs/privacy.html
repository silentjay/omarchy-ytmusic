Privacy Policy

This application is a personal, single-user tool. It accesses your YouTube account data (playlists and liked music) via Google OAuth solely to display and play your own library. Data is never collected, stored on any server, or shared with third parties. Use of data adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. Access can be revoked at any time via your Google Account permissions. Contact: john.mg.allen@gmail.com.
